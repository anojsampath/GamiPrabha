Who we are

A village-rooted start, built in stages

Gami Prabha is a private Sri Lankan company beginning operations in Millaniya with a simple premise: the same coconut, jackfruit, and cinnamon that have grown here for generations can become higher-value food products — and the process of growing and making them is itself worth showing to visitors.

Our first stage is deliberately focused: build small-batch processing capability for three crops, open a working eco-tourism trail through the land that produces them, and prove a model that pays farmers fairly while keeping cultivation organic and low-impact.

How we work

Innovation built for a small first stage

Every method below is chosen because it works at small-batch scale, not because it looks impressive — this is a first-stage company, and the approach reflects that.

Solar drying

Low-energy drying for jackfruit and coconut, cutting fuel cost and preserving flavour better than open flame.

Nothing wasted

Coconut husk and shell become fibre, craft material, and biochar rather than field waste.

Outgrower model

Neighbouring smallholders supply raw crop under a fair, published pricing arrangement.

Organic cultivation

No synthetic pesticide use on land supplying our product lines, verified by our own field checks.
